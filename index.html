My favorite Vietnamese foods

Pho is a traditional Vietnamese noodle soup consisting of broth, rice noodles, herbs, and meat, usually beef or chicken.
Banh Mi is a Vietnamese sandwich made with a crispy baguette, meat, vegetables, and pickled carrots.
Bun Bo Hue is a spicy Vietnamese beef noodle soup made with flavorful broth, beef, herbs, and rice noodles.
Bun Cha is grilled pork served with rice noodles, fresh herbs, and a flavorful dipping sauce.
Cha Gio are crispy Vietnamese spring rolls filled with vegetables, herbs, and meat or seafood.
Com Tam is broken rice served with grilled pork, vegetables, and flavorful sauces.
